eKupi · 2021

Self-service help portal for employees

A digital knowledge and support channel designed to make everyday operations easier.

ProductProcessesDelivery
01

The challenge

Employees needed a more accessible way to find answers, support information and updates about internal applications and retail processes.

02

Why it mattered

Repeated support requests consume time on both sides. A searchable, accessible knowledge hub can reduce unnecessary handoffs.

03

My role

  • ↗Contributed to the definition and rollout of an internal self-service portal
  • ↗Worked across IT support, knowledge sharing and operational workflows
04

What I did

  • ↗Helped establish a portal for FAQs, manuals, business application updates and employee support
  • ↗Connected everyday user questions to a self-service experience
  • ↗Supported the design of processes that reduce repetitive support work
05

What came out of it

  • ↗The published project description reports 40% fewer IT support requests in the following months
  • ↗Created an information channel available to employees across devices on the internal network
06

My takeaway

Sometimes the best digital product is the one that removes friction from dozens of small daily tasks.
