Insurance web & mobile self-service

Connecting customer journeys, accessibility and product delivery in a regulated environment.

ProductPlatformsInsurance
01

The challenge

Insurance self-service experiences involve varied products, user needs and business processes. Changes must remain accessible, consistent and feasible to deliver.

02

Why it mattered

Digital insurance should reduce customer effort rather than simply move complex processes online.

03

My role

  • ↗Product ownership for web and mobile self-service journeys
  • ↗Business and development stakeholder coordination
  • ↗Prioritization of digital improvements
04

What I did

  • ↗Worked on web and mobile customer journeys across insurance products
  • ↗Supported backlog definition and delivery coordination
  • ↗Connected analytics, accessibility and content/SEO considerations with customer experience
  • ↗Collaborated on improvements to the digital purchase and self-service experience
05

What came out of it

  • ↗Supported continued improvement of customer-facing insurance digital channels
06

My takeaway

A digital channel creates value when it makes a real-world task easier for the person using it.
