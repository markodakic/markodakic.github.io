Konzum · 2017–2020

Konzum Online — platform relaunch

Connecting retail operations and technology behind a refreshed online shopping experience.

eCommercePlatformsDelivery
KKonzum / Web + mobile✳
01

The challenge

A large grocery retailer needed an online platform that could support real-world retail, delivery and store operations, not just an attractive storefront.

02

Why it mattered

Grocery eCommerce has operational complexity: availability, delivery, picking, checkout and internal systems must remain aligned.

03

My role

  • ↗Head of eCommerce IT / project lead for the IT scope
  • ↗Defined business and logistics requirements
  • ↗Coordinated internal teams, external vendors and development delivery
04

What I did

  • ↗Helped define business and logistics processes for the refreshed Konzum Online solution
  • ↗Prepared functional documentation for web and mobile features
  • ↗Coordinated technical teams and integration dependencies
  • ↗Connected operational stakeholders and software delivery
05

What came out of it

  • ↗Contributed to the relaunch of the Konzum online shopping platform
  • ↗Helped establish the process and system requirements supporting web and mobile commerce
06

My takeaway

A webshop is the visible tip of a much larger retail operating model.
