eKupi · 2020–2021

Outdoor parcel lockers

From an idea on paper to a physical, digital and logistics service.

eCommerceLogisticsDelivery
01

The challenge

Expanding online shopping meant offering a more flexible way for customers to collect parcels. The challenge was not just to install a cabinet: hardware, custom software, transport, infrastructure and operational processes had to work together.

02

Why it mattered

A collection service only works when the customer journey and the behind-the-scenes logistics process are both reliable.

03

My role

  • ↗Led the initiative from concept through installation
  • ↗Defined project phases, milestones and requirements
  • ↗Coordinated technical, operational and external partners
04

What I did

  • ↗Worked with the manufacturer on hardware, software and outdoor construction requirements for the region
  • ↗Coordinated international transport and site-related implementation activities
  • ↗Defined the logistics processes for the outdoor locker service
  • ↗Coordinated custom software and integration requirements with the eCommerce environment
05

What came out of it

  • ↗Supported implementation of an outdoor collection solution designed for around-the-clock parcel pickup
  • ↗Connected the physical pickup experience with the software and logistics work needed to operate it
06

My takeaway

The real product is the complete service, not only the technology people can see.
